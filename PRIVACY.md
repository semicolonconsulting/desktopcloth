# desktopcloth Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy covers desktopcloth, a macOS app by Semicolon Consulting.

## What desktopcloth collects

Nothing. desktopcloth doesn't collect, send, share or sell any data about you or your Mac. It has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

## Checking for updates

The copy of desktopcloth from the Mac App Store has no network access. The App Store updates it.

The copy downloaded from GitHub can check for a newer version. When you choose Check for Updates, and about once a week unless you turn off Check for Updates Automatically in the menu, it asks GitHub's public API for the latest desktopcloth release. The request says only which version of desktopcloth you have. Like any web request, it reaches GitHub from your internet address; GitHub's own [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) covers that. desktopcloth sends nothing else.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Your settings:** the cloth style you chose, whether the desktop was covered when you quit, whether you've seen the welcome window, and, in the GitHub copy, when it last checked for updates and any version you chose to skip. They're stored in desktopcloth's own sandbox folder on your Mac. If you turn on Open at Login, macOS keeps that in its own list of login items.
- **Your time zone:** for wallpapers that change with the sun, desktopcloth works out where the sun is from your Mac's time zone. It doesn't use Location Services, and nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Wallpaper folder access:** if you allow desktopcloth to read the folder your wallpaper is in, it keeps a bookmark to that folder so it doesn't ask again. It reads the wallpaper only to draw it on your screen. The picture never leaves your Mac.

## Deleting your data

Quit desktopcloth, then move the app and the folder `~/Library/Containers/com.semicolonc.desktopcloth` to the Trash. That removes your settings and the wallpaper folder access.
